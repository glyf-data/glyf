## Contributing

Small contributions are the most welcome: a bug fixed with a test, a clearer
error message, a better example. The
[contributing guide](https://github.com/glyf-data/glyf/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
covers setup, tests and the pull request.

glyf asks contributors to accept a
[Contributor License Agreement](https://github.com/glyf-data/glyf/blob/main/CLA.md).
You keep the copyright in your work. Signing is one comment on your first pull
request, and you only do it once.
